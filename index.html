About

We are approaching a future where intelligence is abundant, but limited by the lack of interaction with and feedback from the physical world. I'm a scientist and engineer working across the hardware and software of optical sensing, imaging, and spectroscopy systems.

My interests are in building the next generation of agent-native instruments, sensors, and imaging systems to accelerate science, medicine, and security.
